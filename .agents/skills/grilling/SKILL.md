# Grilling

Maintain a decision frontier: resolve prerequisite questions before downstream ones.

For every round:
1. Read available evidence first.
2. Ask exactly one high-leverage decision question.
3. State why it matters.
4. Give a recommended answer plus the strongest alternative.
5. After the answer, test it with a concrete scenario or edge case when useful.
6. Move to the next unresolved dependency.

Facts are the agent's job. Decisions are the user's job.

Do not dump a questionnaire. Do not ask questions the repository, current conversation, or primary sources can answer.

Completion criterion: no unresolved ambiguity materially changes scope, domain behavior, architecture direction, or acceptance criteria.
